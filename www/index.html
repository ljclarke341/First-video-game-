HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap left / right side of the screen to change lane.
  • Each gate has three cells. Go through the one whose shape matches yours.
  • The glowing column shows your lane and your current shape.
  • Grab paint blobs to change your shape before the next gate.
  • Striped walls are instant death. They start appearing once you're a dozen gates in.
  • Collect coins to unlock themes. Chain gates for a combo multiplier up to 5x.
