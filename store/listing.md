## Full description (4000 characters max)
```
Three lanes. Three shapes. One thumb.

Every gate ahead of you is split into three cells, and only the one matching
your shape will let you through. Grab a paint blob to change shape, read the
gate, pick your lane — and do it again a fraction of a second sooner as the
course accelerates.

Miss the match and the run is over. Your best score is right there to beat.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Tap the left or right side of the screen to change lane
• Pass through the cell that matches your shape
• Grab paint blobs to switch shape before the next gate
• Dodge the striped walls — they end the run instantly
• Chain gates to build a combo multiplier up to 5x

WHAT'S IN IT
• Endless, procedurally generated courses — no two runs alike
• Six unlockable colour themes, earned by playing
• Combo multipliers that reward clean, committed lines
• Shape glyphs on every gate, so it plays fine if you're colourblind
• Instant restarts — no loading, no waiting
• Plays offline, anywhere

Every course is generated around a guaranteed solution path. If you died, it
was reachable. That one's on you.

Simple to pick up. Genuinely hard to hold a line at full speed.
```
