## What the app stores

The app saves your high score, coin balance, unlocked themes and settings
**on your device only**, using local browser storage inside the app. This data
is never transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

We do not ask for, collect, or transmit your name, email address, contacts,
photos, location, or any account information.

## Advertising

The app displays advertising supplied by **Google AdMob**. To serve ads, AdMob
may collect and process:

- your device's **advertising identifier (Advertising ID)**
- device information such as model, operating system version, and language
- approximate location derived from IP address
- ad interaction data (ads shown, taps)

This is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's privacy
policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in
**Settings → Privacy → Ads** on your Android device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, there is nothing for
us to export or delete. To remove all app data, uninstall the app. For requests
relating to data held by Google AdMob, use the Google link above.
